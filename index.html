Coast FIRE — can I stop contributing?

Coast FIRE = the amount that, invested today and left untouched (no new contributions) at your average real return, grows to the nest egg you need by retirement. Hit it and every future contribution is money you could spend now instead. Average-market, today's dollars; it doesn't run the Monte Carlo behind the reported probability.
